Dual-Support Mechanism with Visual Saliency Guidance and
Auxiliary Acoustic Loss for Image-to-Speech Generation

Chung Tran  ·  Sakriani Sakti  (NAIST)

Image-to-Speech is the task of generating spoken descriptions directly from images, without relying on intermediate text. The model produces natural speech that describes the visual content of an image in an end-to-end fashion, capturing objects, actions, and scene context. This demo presents audio samples generated by our proposed system alongside those from baseline models, evaluated across multiple languages and datasets to demonstrate the model's generalization capability.
